Will Steinlage
Intern, Summer of 2024

“My Engenious internship has assisted my growth as an engineer in more ways than I can count! I learned more with each project I worked on since I was doing real, necessary engineering work, and I never felt overwhelmed thanks to the friendly guidance of my mentor and other coworkers. After this summer I feel significantly more confident in my ability to learn independently as an engineer on the job and to work with a team to solve technical problems.”

Will, a new graduate in electrical engineering, contributed to our team by writing requirements, designing PCBAs, and testing them against specifications. His excellent work over the summer led to a full-time position starting in September 2024.
